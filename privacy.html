Privacy Policy

Rosie Builds respects your privacy. This policy explains the information we may collect when providing our services, including appointment-related SMS messaging, and how we use that information.

Information we collect

We may collect information such as your name, mobile phone number, appointment details, SMS consent status, and responses to appointment-related messages.

How we use information

We use personal information to provide and manage services, schedule appointments, send appointment confirmations and reminders, respond to requests, and operate our messaging and automation systems.

SMS privacy

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

We may use service providers that help us operate appointment scheduling, communications, and SMS delivery. Information is provided to those service providers only as necessary to deliver the relevant service.

Your SMS choices

SMS participation is optional. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP. Message and data rates may apply.

Data retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, comply with applicable obligations, and maintain business records. We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or our SMS program, contact Rosie Builds using the contact information provided when you receive or schedule services with us.
